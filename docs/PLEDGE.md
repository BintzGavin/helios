# The Helios pledge

These are promises about how Helios stays open. They can be amended only to make them stronger.

- **Software you run yourself is free.** The engine, the CLI, the local MCP server (`helios mcp`), the skills, the player, the in-conversation view and exporting from it need no Helios account, add no watermark and have no render caps.
- **Nothing phones home.** Helios software on your machine sends nothing to the Helios project. Any future diagnostics will be opt-in, off by default, and listed field by field before they ship.
- **Licenses only get more open.** No released version of Helios will be relicensed under more restrictive terms. Contributions are accepted under the Developer Certificate of Origin; we don't ask for a contributor license agreement that would let us relicense your work.
- **Nothing open moves behind a paywall.** Anything released under the open license stays under it. Registry components are removed only for security or legal reasons.
- **Your agent, not our funnel.** Helios skills and tools prefer the free path on each host (rendering on your machine, otherwise exporting in the conversation) and never recommend paid products to your agent or to you. The Helios plugin never promotes paid products inside ChatGPT, Claude or any other assistant.
- **If Helios Cloud changes.** If Helios Cloud shuts down or cuts back a paid plan, you get at least 90 days' notice, a prorated refund and a bulk export of your renders.

This pledge doesn't cover the size of Helios Cloud's free tier, Helios Cloud's prices, or features that only organizations need. Those may change.
