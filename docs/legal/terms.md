## Helios Cloud

### Accounts

You can preview videos without an account. To render in the cloud, you sign in, for example by linking your account from ChatGPT or Claude, or with `helios login`. Keep your sign-in secure. An account is for one person; don't share it or create extra accounts to get more free renders.

### Free use and limits

Helios Cloud includes free renders with limits on how many, how long and how large. The current limits are described at https://heliosrender.com/cloud. They may change. Rendering on your own computer stays free.

### Paid plans

If you buy a paid plan on heliosrender.com, the price, billing period and what the plan includes are shown before you pay.

- Plans renew until you cancel. You can cancel at any time, and the plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for.
