01

The idea

Helios draws a frame from its time. Give it t and it returns the light that exists at that instant, every time, on any machine.

The sun has always been the Helios symbol. The new identity keeps it and makes it mean what the engine does. Twelve rays circle a solid disc. Each ray is one step longer than the one before it, clockwise from twelve o'clock, so the sun reads as a clock face and as a progress ring at the same time. The jump between the longest ray and the shortest one is the playhead, the point where a loop ends and begins again.

The construction follows the trademark suns of the mid-century modern era: one solid shape, one colour, no gradients, no outlines, drawn with a compass and a ruler. Those marks survived photocopiers, embroidery and 1-inch newspaper ads. Ours has to survive a 16-pixel favicon, a monochrome composer icon and a terminal.

Three readings of one shape

  • A sun. The disc and rays are unmistakable at any size.
  • A clock. Twelve rays, twelve o'clock at the top, time running clockwise.
  • A seek bar. Rays grow like a progress ring. Frame zero is the short ray; the last frame is the long one.

Every file in assets/brand/ is written by one script from these numbers. The identity is deterministic, like the renderer.

The mark

Construction

The mark lives on a 100-unit square. The disc is centred at 50,50. Rays are parallel-sided bars, never tapered, so they keep their weight when scaled.

Canvas100 × 100

DirectionClockwise
FillOne colour. No stroke, no gradient.

The gap between disc and rays (8 units) equals the ray width. That is the beat the whole mark is tuned to.

Clear space

Keep a margin of at least one disc diameter (38 units, or 38% of the mark's width) free on every side. Nothing enters it: no type, no edges, no other marks.

Minimum size and the compact cut

The full mark works down to 24 pixels. Below that the twelve rays merge, so the identity ships a compact cut with eight thicker rays and a larger disc. The compact cut is for favicons, composer icons, tab bars and anything under 24 pixels. Never scale the full mark below 24 pixels and never use the compact cut above 32.

Colour versions

Three, and only three. Umbra on paper is the default. Bone on Umbra is the reverse. Solar on Umbra is the signature, used where the brand speaks for itself: app icons, social cards, splash screens. Solar never sits on light backgrounds; it fails contrast there.

03

The wordmark

HELIOS is not typed. It is drawn from the same compass and ruler as the mark.

Six capitals on a cap height of 100 with a stem of 22. H, E, L and I are bars. O is a ring with the same stem. S is two arcs of equal radius on a single centreline, cut with flat terminals. The round letters overshoot the cap line by 1.5 units so they look the same height as the flat ones.

Spacing: 16 between H·E and E·L, 11 before I, 15 either side of O. These are fixed; never re-kern the wordmark.

The wordmark is always set in capitals, always in one colour, and always as the supplied vector. Do not retype it in Jost; the proportions differ. In running text, write "Helios" in the body face, title case, no mark.

04

Lockups

The horizontal lockup is the primary signature. The mark stands 1.4 cap heights tall, centred on the wordmark's x-height line, with a gap of 44 units (two stems) between them. Use it for the docs header, the README, the CLI banner and listings that take a wide logo.

The stacked lockup is for square and portrait spaces: splash screens, stickers, slide title cards. The mark is 2.2 cap heights tall with a gap of 36 units above the wordmark. Both elements are centred on the same axis.

Rules: the mark and wordmark in a lockup are always the same colour. Do not build other arrangements, do not put the wordmark on the left, and do not replace the O with the mark.

Colour

Two neutrals do most of the work. One colour is the sun. Two more are for signals.

Mid-century marks were designed for one ink. So is this one. Umbra is a warm near-black, the colour of a shadow rather than a screen. Bone is unbleached paper. Solar is the sun, saturated and slightly orange, and it is a fill colour, not a text colour, on light backgrounds.

Typography

Three faces, three jobs. A geometric display face that shares the wordmark's bones, a text face built for screens, and a mono for everything the engine says.

Frame 1440 of 1800, rendered in 11 ms.
Give it a time. It gives you the light.
Helios is a deterministic renderer. A composition is one HTML page that draws any frame from its time t, which means the same frame comes out of a laptop, a Lambda and a browser tab, pixel for pixel, with no state carried between them.
$ helios render composition.html --fps 30 --duration 60 --out launch.mp4
RoleFaceWeightsWhere
DisplayJost500, 600Headlines, section titles, numbers that are the point. Never below 22 px.

MonoIBM Plex Mono400, 500Code, CLI output, timecodes, frame counts, eyebrows and captions.

Jost is a Futura revival, which puts the headlines in the same family as the mark: circles, straight stems, flat terminals. Plex was drawn for engineering documentation, and that is what most Helios writing is. Both are open licensed and served by Google Fonts, so they work in the docs, the plugin view and an MCP App iframe without any build step.

07

Motion

The mark has exactly one move: it seeks. Rays appear clockwise from twelve o'clock, one per step, and stop.

The seek is a step function, not an ease. Each ray switches on, it does not fade or grow. At 30 frames per second the full seek takes 24 frames: 2 frames per ray, then the mark holds. Reverse it to wind out. Loop it only in a loading state, with a 12-frame hold on the full sun.

Everything else in the brand moves the same way the renderer does: driven by time, never by physics. Fades are linear and 6 frames. Elements enter in place; nothing slides, bounces or overshoots. Reduced-motion settings get the final frame immediately.

// one frame of the mark, like any Helios composition
function drawMark(t, fps = 30) {
  const step = Math.floor(t * fps / 2);   // 2 frames per ray
  return Math.min(12, step);              // rays lit, 0..12
}

Applications

Docs and README

The docs site uses the horizontal lockup at 26 pixels tall in the header, Umbra in light mode and Bone in dark mode. Noon is the link colour, Solar Deep is reserved for the current nav item.

Getting started · Guides · Examples · API

Terminal

The CLI banner sets the wordmark in text, in capitals with the mark drawn from block characters, and prints in the terminal's default foreground with Solar used only for the sun. It never clears the screen.

Studio and the player

Studio is a dark surface: Umbra background, ink-800 panels, ink-700 rules, Bone text, Solar for the playhead and the active frame. Noon marks selection. Ember marks dropped frames and failed renders. The player's scrubber uses the same seek mark as a loading spinner, and nothing else spins.

10

Voice

Helios speaks like a good engineer explaining a tool they built: plainly, specifically, and without selling.

  • Accurate first. Helios is a deterministic renderer that calls no generative model. Say so. Never imply it generates footage.
  • Concrete over clever. Numbers, file names and commands beat adjectives. "1800 frames in 41 seconds" beats "blazing fast".
  • Short sentences. One idea each. Active voice. No exclamation marks.
  • Web standards are the hero. The pitch is that the browser already knows how to draw; Helios adds time.
  • No upsell in hosts. Inside Claude, ChatGPT or Codex, Helios states limits and links to heliosrender.com. No prices, plan names or prompts to upgrade.
Write this
Helios renders one HTML page into video. The page draws any frame from its time, so rendering can start at frame 1200 on one machine and frame 0 on another, and the output stitches without re-encoding.
Not this
Helios is a revolutionary AI-powered video platform that lets you create stunning cinematic content in seconds. Unleash your creativity today!

Naming in text: Helios, Helios Studio, the Helios CLI, the Helios player, the Helios registry. Package names stay in code: @helios-project/core. The tagline is "Video is light over time." It is a sentence, with a full stop, and it is the only tagline.
