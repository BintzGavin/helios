# Animated video philosophy

Read [philosophy.md](philosophy.md) start to finish before giving creative direction, critiquing a cut, or structuring scenes. That file is the verbatim working philosophy.

## How to apply it

- Gate work on the five questions at the end of `philosophy.md`. If they are not clear, prioritize script and intent over more keyframes.
- When reviewing builds, check **IX. The technical commitments** and **XI. What I won't do** explicitly.
- When timing or rhythm feels wrong, use **VI** (including the ~30 viewings rule) before tuning easing alone.
