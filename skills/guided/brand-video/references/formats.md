# Format profiles

Defaults apply when the user doesn't say otherwise. "Short" means at or under the profile's short threshold. Stagger ranges are for motion-design-rules Rule 4 and apply per word, line or element; letters run faster (15–35 ms). A style card's timing overrides them. When a short runtime can't fit an entrance at these speeds, shorten the text rather than rushing every scene. Whatever the ending, the final CTA or install line stays fully readable for at least 1.5 s.

## promo — promotional or hype video

- Default 30 s, 16:9. Short ≤ 15 s: punch and clarity over completeness.
- Music: high energy, following the brand's emotional arc.
- Timing: scene changes on beats, key claims on downbeats.
- Stagger 50–80 ms.

## explainer — how it works, walkthrough, educational

- Default 60 s, 16:9. Short ≤ 30 s: Hook → Core concept → CTA.
- Extra brand signals: docs structure, feature descriptions, onboarding flows, help text, information architecture.
- Arc: Hook (first 5 s) → Problem → Solution → Step by step → Summary and CTA. At most 5 sections, each with a clear on-screen header and step marker.
- Music: calm and steady, never competing with narration. Section changes on downbeats, text reveals on beats.
- Each key message stays on screen at least 3 s. Stagger 100–150 ms.

## demo — product demo, UI walkthrough, feature showcase

- Default 45 s, 16:9. Short ≤ 30 s: at most 2 features, strongest differentiator first.
- Extra brand signals: UI components, page layouts and routes, feature flags or folders, API endpoints, dashboards, settings, README feature lists, screenshots. Build a feature map: top 3–5 features, main workflow, key surfaces, differentiators.
- Arc: brand moment (2–3 s) → per feature: context (wide), detail (zoom on the interaction), result (benefit visible) → CTA with product name. At most 5 features, at least 5 s each; never a feature without its benefit.
- Rebuild real UI faithfully. Highlight with the card's emphasis device. Feature reveals on downbeats. Stagger 80–120 ms.

## testimonial — social proof, reviews

- Default 30 s, 16:9. Short ≤ 15 s: 1 quote + 1 stat + CTA, strongest quote.
- Extra brand signals: testimonial and review components, ratings, customer and partner logos, trust badges, counters.
- Arc: context moment (2–3 s) → quotes with attribution → proof stats → CTA with brand mark. At most 3 quotes, each readable for at least 4 s.
- Only real quotes from the repo or the user. If there are none, build the structure with clearly labelled placeholders; never invent a quote.
- Music: warm, gentle build. Quote reveals on downbeats. Stagger 100–150 ms.

## launch — launch, release, reveal

- Default 30 s, 16:9. Short ≤ 15 s: skip the tension act, open on the reveal, at most 2 features.
- Extra brand signals: changelog and release notes, version history, recent features, product name and tagline. Note the size of the launch (v1 or point release).
- Arc: Tension (30%) → Reveal (20%, on the music's biggest hit) → Showcase (35%, features one after another) → CTA (15%, availability and sign-off). At most 5 features.
- Version numbers and dates are prominent. The reveal is the peak: use the card's native move, not a generic flash. Stagger 80–120 ms.

## social — Reels, TikTok, Shorts

- Default 15 s, 9:16 at 1080×1920. Decline anything over 60 s.
- Extra brand signals: short-form hooks, social handles.
- Arc: hook in the first 1–2 s (no logo intro, no build-up) → one idea per beat → payoff and CTA in the last 2–3 s, looping back to the first frame.
- Music: immediate hook, punchy, loop-friendly. Every visual change lands on a beat.
- Type: at most 5 words per screen, at least 72 px, readable in under a second. Keep text out of the top 10% and bottom 15% (platform UI). Transitions are immediate. Stagger 50–80 ms.
