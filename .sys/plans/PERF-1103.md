# PERF-1103: Merge multi-worker `runWorker` chunk loops

## Focus Area
The multi-worker loop in `CaptureLoop.ts` (`packages/renderer/src/core/CaptureLoop.ts`), specifically the `runWorker` function around lines 407-476.

## Background Research
Currently, inside the `runWorker` logic of `CaptureLoop.ts`, we maintain two largely identical multi-worker chunk loops based on `isDomStrategy`.
Lines 415-448 and lines 450-474 consist of very similar AST logic: checking worker condition, fetching time, then calling capture functions.

By merging these branches and relying on hoisting the `domBeginFrame` vs `strategy.capture` distinction inside the `runWorker` block natively, we can reduce the overall AST complexity of the module.

## Implementation Spec

### Step 1: Fold Multi-worker Writer Chunk Loops

**What to change**:
