# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in contributing to Helios.

## Developer Certificate of Origin

Helios accepts contributions under the [Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1](https://developercertificate.org/). Sign off every commit to certify that you wrote it or have the right to submit it under the project's license:

    git commit -s -m "your message"

This adds a `Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>` line. We don't use a contributor license agreement.
